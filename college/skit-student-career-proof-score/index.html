Career proof score for SKIT students

The main focus of this page is career proof for SKIT Jaipur students. A student resume should clearly show role direction, technical or practical skills, project evidence, education, contact clarity and improvement areas. A good resume is not only about design. It should answer three questions quickly: what role is this student targeting, what skills does the student have, and where is the proof?

The goal is not to replace placement guidance or personal mentoring. The goal is to give SKIT students a quick, structured and practical first check before they send the same resume everywhere.

Common resume problem

Many freshers mention multiple skills such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, WordPress, Java, React, SQL or AI tools, but the resume does not clearly show where those skills were used. Recruiters may see keywords, but they may not see proof.

The second common issue is role confusion. A student may write web development, coding, AI, communication and management together, but the resume may not clearly show whether the student is ready for frontend, WordPress, PHP, full stack, internship or general fresher roles.

What the tool checks

Resume clarity

Checks whether the resume is readable, focused and easy to scan.

Skill proof

Compares skills mentioned with project evidence visible in the resume.

Project proof

Looks for project descriptions, GitHub, live links, deployment and real-world features.

Role fit

Suggests whether the resume looks stronger for frontend, WordPress, PHP, full stack or fresher roles.

The output is designed as a diagnosis, not a generic motivational answer. It highlights strong points, missing points, improvement areas and a practical next step.

Sample score explanation

Example: if you mentioned 12 technical skills but only 5 are clearly visible through projects, the report may show a Skill Proof Gap. This helps you understand that simply listing skills is not enough.

Another example: if your resume has a project title but no GitHub, no live link and no feature explanation, the tool can mark project proof as weak and suggest adding deployment or feature-level evidence.
