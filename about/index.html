Academic & Technical Profile

Bringing real-world software engineering into college guest lectures

I combine computer science teaching with hands-on work in web applications, e-commerce, APIs, cloud deployment and AI-assisted engineering. Colleges and universities can invite me for practical, department-coordinated technical sessions.

About Me

Academic teaching supported by practical implementation experience

My background includes B.Tech and M.Tech qualifications in Computer Science Engineering, academic teaching and software development. As a faculty member and developer, I focus on connecting classroom concepts to real systems: how applications are planned, built, integrated, debugged and deployed.

For an invited session, I work with the host department to align the discussion with its students, syllabus and desired learning outcomes.

Technical session themes

  • Web application design and project development
  • E-commerce systems, Magento and WordPress
  • REST APIs, integrations and payment workflows
  • Cloud hosting, deployment and production readiness
  • Responsible AI-assisted coding and debugging
  • Research topic selection and project planning

For Colleges & Universities

Guest lectures, practical workshops and faculty sessions

Sessions are arranged with a college, university, department or authorized faculty coordinator. The format and technical depth can be adapted for B.Tech, BCA, MCA, postgraduate or faculty audiences.

Guest Lectures

Focused technical talks connecting classroom concepts with real development and deployment practices.

Hands-on Workshops

Faculty Development Sessions

Invite me for an institutional session

Faculty coordinators and department representatives can discuss the topic, audience, proposed duration and online or offline format. Invitations are coordinated through the host institution, not through individual student enrolments.

I do not offer paid individual coaching or academic advantage to students whom I currently teach, supervise or assess.
