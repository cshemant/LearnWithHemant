Selection rule

  • Avoid generic topics like “AI in Education”.
  • Use a narrow title with dataset, method and outcome.
  • Choose topics that can produce papers and implementation proof.

Important

How to finalize one topic

Pick the topic only if you can answer: What exact problem am I solving? What dataset will I use? What model will be compared? What is my new contribution?

Good title“Explainable ML for skill-gap prediction among CSE students.”
Weak title“Research on artificial intelligence in education.”
Good contributionImproved accuracy plus explainability and actionable recommendations.
Weak contributionOnly applying an existing algorithm without a clear gap.
