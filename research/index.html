Research Workflow

Choose a problem, not just a technology

A good CSE PhD topic should be narrow, measurable, publishable and linked with available data or implementation proof.

01

Select Area

Pick AI/ML, cybersecurity, cloud, software engineering, recommender systems or education analytics.

02

Find Gap

Compare 10–15 recent papers and identify what is missing: accuracy, explainability, data quality, cost or deployment.

03

Plan Method

Define dataset, baseline model, proposed model, evaluation metrics and expected contribution.

04

Prepare Proposal

Convert the idea into title, objectives, methodology, timeline and references for PhD admission interview.
